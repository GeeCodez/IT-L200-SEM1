# RISK MANAGEMENT

## CLASSIFICATION OF RISKS
1. **Project Risks**:
    - These are related to budgetary, schedule, personnel, resources and customer-related problems. eg: schedule slippage.

2. **Technical Risks**: mostly occur due to the teams insuffienct knowledge about the project.
    - They concern potential methods, implementation,interfacing, testing and maintenance.
    -It also consists of ambiguous, incomplete and changing specification, technical uncertainty and technical obsolescence.

3. **Business Risks**: building something that nobody needs.

### OTHER CATEGORIES OF RISKS
1. Known risks: risks that can be uncovered after careful assesment of the project and related activities.

2. Predictable risks: risks that are hypothesized from previous projects experience.

3. Unpredictable risks: risks that can and do occur but are hard to identify in advance.

## PRINCIPLES OF RISK MANAGEMENT
* **Global perspective**: analyze the whole system to understand the probability and the impact of the risks.

* **Take a forward-looking view**: Consider future risks and plan ahead of them.

* **Open communication**: there should be free sharing of risk information among team members and clients.

* **Integrated management**: risk management should be made an integral part of the poject management.

* **Continuos process**: continuous tracking and management of risks.

## RISKS MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES
* Risk management consists of:
    - Risk assesment (Identification, Analysis and Prioritization)
    - Risk Control (Management planning, monitoring and resolution)

### RISK ASSESSMENT 
* Objective: to divide the risks in the condition of their loss causing potential.
* Every risks is rated in 2 ways: 
    - Possibility of coming true (r).
    - Severity of possible damage it could cause (s).
    - **priority(p)=r*s**
* Most likely and damaging risks are controlled first.

#### 1. RISK IDENTIFICATION 
* The process of finding risks early in a project in order to make plans to reduce their impact. Can be categorized into the following: 
    - Technology risks
    - People risks.
    - Organizational risks.
    - Tools risks.
    - Requirements risk: from changing requirements.
    - Estimation risks.

#### 2. RISK ANALYSIS
* Evaluating each identified risk to determine its probability and seriousness (impact).
* Based mainly on experience and judgment from past projects.
* Could be in one of the following bands: 
    - Very low(0-10%)
    - Low (10-20%)
    - Moderate (25-50%)
    - High ( 50-75 %)
    - Very high (75%+)
* The effect of the risk might be determined as catastrophic (threaten the survival of the plan), serious (would cause significant delays), tolerable (delays are within allowed contingency), or insignificant.

#### 3. RISK CONTROL
* Managing risks to achieve desired outcome. It requires ingenuity on the part of the project manager. 
    - Avoiding the risk: changing requirements, incentivizing humans to reduce human resource turnover.
    - Transfer of risks: getting the risky element deveveloped by a third party or buying insurance.
    - Risk Reduction: planning method to reduce the impact of a risks.

* **Risk Leverage**: the variation in risk exposure divided by the amount of reducing the risk.
    - Risk Leverage=(Exposure before reduction - Exposure after reduction )/(Cost of reduction).

#### 1. RISK PLANNING:
* Developing ways to cope with identified risks with minimal disruption to the project.

#### RISK MONITORING
* Continuously checking the risks to check if something has changed. Done in order to update the risk plan or activities.
