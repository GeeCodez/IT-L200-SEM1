# Society and Lifestyle: Technology and Attitudes

## 1. Uncritical Euphoric Stance
An overly positive or enthusiastic attitude toward something without questioning it. 

### Key Points
- **Technology:** Seen as solving problems and improving life.  
- **Society / Knowledge:**
  - More informed society  
  - Enables complex learning  
  - Encourages multitasking  
- **Economy / Jobs:**
  - Cheaper prices  
  - Greater specialization in jobs  
  - Rise of a leisure class  
- **Social / Networking:**
  - Global networking  
  - Denser social circles  
- **Risks / Downsides:** Often overlooked (e.g., overload from multitasking, uncritical enthusiasm)
---

## 2. Hypercritical / Pessimistic Stance
Tendency to be overly negative and critical/doubtful about something often assuming the worst without acknowledgeing potential benefits.

### Key Points
- **Technicism:** Belief that technology can solve all problems, sometimes ignoring ethics, social, and environmental issues.  
- **New technological dangers:** Nuclear reactors, chemical plants → accidents and disasters.  
- **Social effects of new entertainment:** Video games, internet → may affect academic performance and social skills.  
- **Health risks:** Sedentary lifestyles → obesity and other disorders.  
- **Social separation:** Technology increases speed of communication → reduces deep human interaction.  
- **Economic risks:** Structural unemployment due to automation and technological progress.  
- **Environmental risks:** Pollution, industrial accidents, anthropogenic climate change. 
---

## 3. Comparison: Euphoric vs Pessimistic Stance

| Aspect | Euphoric (Positive) | Pessimistic (Negative) |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| **Attitude** | Enthusiastic, assumes benefits | Critical, assumes risks |
| **Technology** | Solves problems, improves life | Creates dangers, accidents, pollution |
| **Society / Knowledge** | Informed, complex learning | Overreliance on tech (technicism), ignores ethical/social issues |
| **Economy / Jobs** | Cheaper prices, specialization, leisure class | Structural unemployment |
| **Social / Networking** | Global networking, denser social circles | Social separation, shallow interactions |
| **Health / Lifestyle** | Increased efficiency and convenience | Obesity, disorders, mental health issues |
| **Environmental Impact** | Often positive (better access, efficiency) | Pollution, climate change, disasters |
---

**Tip to remember:**  
- Euphoric = “Everything new is wonderful!”  
- Pessimistic = “Everything new is risky!”  
- Use **DANGER** for pessimistic risks:  
  - **D**isasters  
  - **A**utomation (job loss)  
  - **N**egative health  
  - **G**lobal environment  
  - **E**ntertainment/social effects  
  - **R**eliance on technology (technicism)

## DIGITAL SOCIETY
* **Digital society**: is a society where the creation, distribution, use, integration and manipulation of information is a significant economic, political and cultural activity.

* People in such societies are called **DIGITAL CITIZENS**.

* **Information Society**: a society in which a majority of the labor force is composed of information workers and in which information is the most important element.
* Sharp chang of the **Industrial society** in which key element was energy.

* **Information workers** are individuals whose main activity is producing, processing or distributing information and producing information technology.
* **Information**: patterned matter-energy that affects that probabilites to an individual's decision.
    - not physical, expressed in material/energy form. can be traded.
